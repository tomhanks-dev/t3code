name: t3-personal-fork
description: Compatibility entry point for personal T3 feature requests and upstream integration; routes to the maintained project-local fork skills.
---

Read `FORK.md` from the current candidate. For features and fixes, follow
[t3-fork-feature](../t3-fork-feature/SKILL.md). For upstream merges and feature
overlap, follow [t3-fork-upstream](../t3-fork-upstream/SKILL.md). Feature work ends ready on its feature branch. For an explicit release,
merge-and-build request, or installation/cleanup status, follow [t3-fork-release](../t3-fork-release/SKILL.md).
The operational procedure is in `docs/operations/personal-fork.md`.
